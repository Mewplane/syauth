# BUG-20261107-0012: Controller loss leaves a running but unusable daemon

## Summary

The daemon retained a removed BlueZ controller, logged advertisement failures,
and continued serving status as up while authentication could not reach the phone.

## Reproduction

On the affected Fedora laptop, the process started November 1. Advertisement
rotation failed from November 4 at 17:53 with
`target object was either not present or removed`. On November 7, BlueZ exposed
`hci1` with zero active advertisements while RPC still reported `state: up`.
A managed-service restart restored advertising and a phone subscription. A fresh
sudo challenge at 00:14:07.930 completed successfully at 00:14:10.549.

The kernel separately logged an `hci0` MediaTek coredump on November 6 at
20:09:46 and `hci1` initialization seven seconds later. This later reset does
not establish the cause of the first November 4 advertisement failure.

## Expected

Controller loss invalidates transport readiness. The managed service rebuilds
GATT and advertising on the available controller with the existing bond. Phone
absence or an unanswered approval does not invalidate local transport health.

## Actual

The rotation loop warned indefinitely. Startup controller selection did not
repair a controller handle invalidated after construction.

## Root Cause

PersistentPeripheral retained its original Adapter for its lifetime. The
orchestrator only logged advertisement failures. Neither runtime supervision
nor status distinguished Bluetooth health from RPC liveness. BlueZ setup
failure also left a socket-only process instead of requesting recovery.

## Fix

- Subscribe to controller removal events and latch loss even if its name returns.
- Probe controller power and active advertising every two seconds, serialized
  against advertisement replacement. Bound the probe to one second.
- Track successful advertisement publication separately from daemon liveness.
- Drain and exit with failure after transport loss or initialization failure.
  The service retries every two seconds with no restart limit, selecting the
  available default controller and loading the existing bond from disk.
- Add optional `transport_ready` to Status RPC and CLI JSON. Absence means
  unknown for older daemons. The field does not report phone presence.
- Bound orchestrator shutdown so a stalled Bluetooth call cannot block recovery.

## Traceability

- `tests/lifecycle_smoke.rs::unavailable_bluez_exits_for_supervised_recovery`
  failed before startup transport errors propagated.
- `tests/rotation.rs` covers readiness before advertising, healthy advertising
  without a phone, failed updates, and controller loss before minute rotation.
- `tests/transport_recovery.rs` drives the real daemon on a private D-Bus with
  BlueZ interfaces, removes hci0, observes failure exit and socket cleanup, then
  relaunches on hci1 and checks bond hydration and unchanged bond/key bytes.
- `peripheral/health.rs` covers controller name reuse, unrelated removal, and
  event-stream termination.
- RPC roundtrip and legacy decode tests cover wire compatibility. CLI tests
  preserve transport failure independently of `daemon.state: up`.
- Display consumer: sibling sy `BUG-20261107-0012` and sy-auth applet regressions.

## Validation

The isolated PR worktree passes `make lint` and `make test`, with 460 passing
test results, zero failures, and 10 ignored. The full host working tree also
passes with 486 results, including independent PAM work outside this change.

The native RPM was installed on November 7. The managed service started at
01:36:09, advertised at 01:36:11, and the phone subscribed at 01:36:17. A fresh
sudo challenge completed through phone approval at 01:36:33.173–01:36:35.308.
`rpm -V syauth` passes. All seven bond/key files retain their original contents,
ownership, and private modes. Physical removal is covered on the private bus;
normal advertising and authentication are verified on the live host.
